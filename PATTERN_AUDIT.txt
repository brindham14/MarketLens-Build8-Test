MARKETLENS PATTERN AUDIT — BUILD 3

Changes in this build:
- One shared pattern flag source is used by the Screener, Popular Scans and stock detail view.
- Screener now has Cup & Handle, Flag, Pocket Pivot and Darvas breakout filters in addition to VCP and generic pivot breakout.
- Pattern detail distinguishes Candidate, Forming, Recent Breakout and Not Qualifying where the uploaded fields support that status.
- Generic pivot breakout remains separate from Cup & Handle, Flag and Darvas breakout fields.
- VCP volume dry-up no longer treats zero/invalid volume as evidence of contraction.
- Flag and Cup & Handle geometry uses completed bars, reserving the latest bar for breakout detection so a breakout bar cannot replace the prior pivot.
- Cup & Handle logic still checks numerical depth/duration/rim/handle conditions; it cannot prove a smooth rounded U. Visual chart confirmation remains required.
